# Refund, Resolution & Data Protection Policy  
For Customers Using Pavodah

---

## Purpose of This Policy
This policy explains, in clear terms, how Pavodah handles:
- Refunds
- Service issues and dispute resolution
- Customer data collection, storage, and protection

This policy applies to **customers only** and is written to help customers understand their rights and responsibilities while using the Pavodah app.

---

## 1. Refund Policy

### 1.1 General Refund Rule
Payments made on Pavodah are **held securely** until a service is completed or resolved.

A customer may be eligible for a refund if:
- The service provider fails to show up
- The service is not delivered as agreed
- The provider abandons the job without explanation
- The service quality is clearly below what was agreed upon
- The provider violates Pavodah safety rules

Refunds are **not automatic** and depend on review of the issue.

---

### 1.2 Situations Where Refunds May NOT Apply
Refunds may not be approved if:
- The customer confirms the service as completed
- The issue is caused by incomplete or incorrect information provided by the customer
- The customer requests extra work outside the agreed booking
- The service was delivered correctly but the customer later changes their mind

---

### 1.3 How Customers Can Request a Refund
To request a refund, customers must:
1. Report the issue through the Pavodah in-app chat
2. Clearly explain the problem
3. Provide supporting information where possible (messages, photos, etc.)

Customers are advised **not to contact providers privately** outside the app, as this may affect refund eligibility.

---

### 1.4 Refund Method
Approved refunds will be processed through:
- The same **Mobile Money** account used for payment, or
- The same **bank card** used during checkout

Refund processing time may vary depending on the payment provider.

---

## 2. Service Resolution & Dispute Handling

### 2.1 Resolution First Approach
Pavodah encourages resolution before escalation.

Customers may:
- Request clarification
- Ask for revisions
- Report delays or concerns through the app chat

Many issues can be resolved quickly when reported early.

---

### 2.2 Raising a Dispute
A dispute can be raised if:
- The provider fails to deliver the service
- The service outcome is significantly different from what was agreed
- There is a serious communication or conduct issue

Once a dispute is raised:
- The order may be temporarily paused
- Evidence from both sides may be reviewed
- A fair resolution decision will be made

---

### 2.3 Resolution Outcomes
Possible outcomes include:
- Service completion request
- Partial refund
- Full refund
- Order cancellation

Each case is reviewed individually to ensure fairness.

---

- Customers must **not share personal phone numbers, WhatsApp, or email**
- Payments must never be made outside Pavodah

Failure to follow these rules may limit Pavodah’s ability to assist in disputes or refunds.
